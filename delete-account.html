Delete Your JNVST GURU Account

JNVST GURU allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your JNVST GURU account, send an email to:

Email: srtech.education@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your JNVST GURU account. In your request, clearly state that you want your JNVST GURU account and associated data deleted.

What Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, the account and associated personal and learning data will be deleted in accordance with our data retention and deletion practices.

This may include account information, student profile information, practice and learning data, attempts, answers, scores, and related account-associated data.

Deletion Requests

We may need to verify the request to protect the account and prevent unauthorized deletion. After verification, the deletion request will be processed by the JNVST GURU team.

Questions

If you have any questions about account or data deletion, contact us at:

srtech.education@gmail.com

For more information about how JNVST GURU collects, uses, and protects user information, please see our Privacy Policy.
