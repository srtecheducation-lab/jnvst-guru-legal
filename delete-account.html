Delete Your JNVST GURU Account

JNVST GURU allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data. Users can request account deletion directly through the app or by contacting our support team.

How to Delete Your Account Through the App

You can request deletion of your JNVST GURU account by following these steps:

  1. Open the JNVST GURU app.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the information and select Confirm Deletion.

Follow the confirmation instructions displayed in the app to complete your account deletion request.

How to Request Account Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by sending an email to:

Email: srtech.education@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your JNVST GURU account. Clearly state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, the account and associated personal and learning data will be deleted in accordance with our data retention and deletion practices.

This may include:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Student profile information.
  • Practice and learning history.
  • Practice attempts and submitted answers.
  • Scores and progress information.
  • Other data associated with the account.

Deletion Requests and Verification

We may need to verify the deletion request to protect your account and prevent unauthorized deletion. Once verified, the request will be processed by the JNVST GURU team.

Questions

If you have any questions about account or data deletion, contact us at:

srtech.education@gmail.com

For more information about how JNVST GURU collects, uses, and protects user information, please see our Privacy Policy.
