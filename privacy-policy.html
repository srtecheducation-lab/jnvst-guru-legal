JNVST GURU Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

JNVST GURU ("we", "our", "us", or "the app") is an educational application developed by MS Tech Education to help students prepare for the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST).

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is protected, how it may be processed by service providers, and how users can request deletion of their account and associated information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is necessary to provide account, authentication, student profile, educational, practice, and learning-related functionality.

1.1 Account Information

When a user creates or uses a JNVST GURU account, we may collect account-related information such as:

  • Email address
  • Authentication information associated with the account

Authentication may be provided through email/password authentication or supported third-party authentication services such as Google.

1.2 Student Profile Information

When a student profile is created, the application collects the following profile information:

  • Name
  • Date of birth
  • Gender
  • Category
  • Residential area (Rural or Urban)
  • Class level
  • State
  • District
  • Preferred language
  • Exam session

These student profile fields are required for creating the student profile in the current application.

The application may validate certain profile information, including the student's date of birth, class level, and preferred language, according to the application's requirements.

1.3 Learning and Practice Information

When students use the educational and practice features of JNVST GURU, the application may collect and store information related to their learning activity, including:

  • Questions attempted
  • Selected answers
  • Practice attempts
  • Scores and results
  • Correct and incorrect responses
  • Unanswered questions
  • Practice mode
  • Subject and topic information
  • Difficulty information
  • Learning and performance information

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide educational and practice features.
  • Maintain student profiles.
  • Provide practice results and learning information.
  • Record practice activity and learning progress.
  • Associate students with the relevant JNVST exam session.
  • Provide appropriate language preferences.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the application.
  • Analyze student performance and learning outcomes.
  • Generate educational and statistical insights.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.

3. Educational and Statistical Analysis

JNVST GURU may use student profile information and learning activity to understand educational performance and learning outcomes.

This analysis may include information such as category, residential area (Rural or Urban), preferred language, class level, state, district, and other information associated with a student's educational profile.

Such information may be used to analyze student performance across different educational and demographic categories and to generate aggregated educational or statistical insights.

In particular, category and residential-area information may be used for analysis of student performance and outcomes in relation to characteristics that may be relevant to JNVST preparation and reservation-related categories.

These analyses are intended to support educational understanding, reporting, and future educational features. Such information is not intended to discriminate against students or independently determine a student's eligibility for admission or reservation.

4. Authentication and Third-Party Services

JNVST GURU uses third-party services to provide authentication, backend, hosting, storage, security, and other technical functionality.

4.1 Supabase

JNVST GURU uses Supabase for authentication and backend services. Account and application data may therefore be processed and stored using Supabase infrastructure.

Supabase processes information according to its applicable terms and privacy practices.

4.2 Google Sign-In

JNVST GURU may provide Google Sign-In as an authentication option. When Google Sign-In is used, authentication is processed through Google's authentication services.

Google may process information according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide the application's authentication, hosting, storage, security, and technical functionality.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of users, the application, or others.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data transmitted between the application and supported backend services may use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

JNVST GURU is an educational application intended for students, including school-age children.

We aim to collect and use information that is reasonably necessary for providing account, authentication, educational, practice, and learning functionality.

Parents or guardians who have questions about information associated with a child may contact us using the contact information provided below.

Parents or guardians may also contact us to request deletion of an account and associated personal information.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain accounts, provide educational functionality, comply with applicable legal requirements, and resolve disputes or support requests.

When an account and associated data are deleted, we will take reasonable steps to remove associated personal information, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain under applicable law.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their JNVST GURU account and associated personal information by contacting us at:

MS Tech Education
Email: srtech.education@gmail.com

A deletion request should include the email address associated with the JNVST GURU account so that we can identify the relevant account.

Account deletion requests are processed through our backend administration process.

When a deletion request is processed, we will take reasonable steps to delete the account and associated personal information, subject to applicable legal, security, and technical requirements.

10. User Choices and Privacy Requests

Users should provide information that is accurate and appropriate for their use of JNVST GURU.

Users and parents or guardians may contact us regarding questions about information associated with an account or to request account and data deletion.

Privacy-related requests can be submitted using the contact email provided in this Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, our data practices, third-party services, or applicable legal and platform requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
